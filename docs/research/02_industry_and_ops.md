## FairAppeal
- Fully managed homeowner appeals; "AI-native home advisor, starting with property taxes". Homeowner does not appear at hearings. https://www.fairappeal.com/is-fairappeal-legit ; https://www.fairappeal.com/careers/founders-associate
- Fee 25% of first-year savings, no fee if unsuccessful. https://fairappeal.com
- Example boards named: King County WA BOE, Cuyahoga OH BOR, NY town Boards of Assessment Review. Careers lists contract Consultant/Representative roles in all 50 states. https://www.fairappeal.com/careers
- Hearings presented by contract "Property Tax Advisors", paid per case, remote; FairAppeal builds the case/evidence; advisor presents, negotiates, reports outcome. "Most hearings are virtual or by phone." https://www.fairappeal.com/careers/property-tax-advisor
- Founder's Associate: "own our property-tax operation", "build the tools that make it increasingly self-driving"; coordinating local advisors, deadlines, checking submissions, following up on decisions. $70-100k + 0.25-0.3% equity, Brooklyn.
- SEC Form D: FairAppeal, Inc., $1,504,000 from 6 investors, first sale 2026-08-24. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2154028/000215402826000001/xslFormDX01/primary_doc.xml

## Coordination practice analogs
- CA courts interpreter scheduling RFP 2026: SMS/email alerts, accept/decline by text reply, status lifecycle, auto-matching with override, fill rate/response time/no-show reporting. https://courts.ca.gov/system/files?file=solicitation-request-document%2Frfp-lss-2026-207-rb-exhibit-1-requirements.pdf
- Instawork (company-reported): <2% no-shows, 97% fill in 24h; preferred-worker rosters. https://research.contrary.com/company/instawork
- Science (NYC): redesigned summons + text reminders cut failure-to-appear 13-21%. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33033154/
- Cochrane (8 RCTs, n=6,615): SMS reminders vs none RR 1.14 (78.6% vs 67.8% attendance); SMS ~ phone calls; SMS 55-65% cheaper per attendance. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24310741/
- "98% SMS open rate" stats are untraced marketing claims; do not use.
