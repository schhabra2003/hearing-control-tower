# What happens when nobody attends a hearing

What a missed hearing costs depends on the rule of the office. This file collects the rules that were found, with a link and a quoted phrase for each.

## Summary

Non-appearance does not have one outcome: it ranges from dismissal to a decision on the papers, and in most places an affidavit or written-evidence election removes the risk for little cost. I found no published no-show rate anywhere, so that number cannot go in the business case from this research.

All quotes were read through a page-fetch tool that summarises; spot-check the exact wording against the source before it goes into a client-facing document.

Three points matter most for the operating plan:

- **Texas agents carry more risk than owners.** The one no-cause postponement is only for owners without an agent, and remote hearings need 10 days' written notice when an agent is designated. Travis dismisses agent protests not completed by the Friday of the following week.
- **The cheap protections are paperwork, not people.** File an evidence affidavit on every Texas protest, elect written-evidence review where offered, and calendar the reinstatement window (4 days in Texas, up to 60 in California, before the decision issues in Florida).
- **Cook County is closed to a consultant model.** Its rules bar non-attorneys from representing taxpayers before the Board of Review.

The only Ohio rule I could read is one level up: the state Board of Tax Appeals dismisses for failure to prosecute when the appellant misses the hearing.

| 8 | Texas: an affidavit is considered only if the owner does not appear in person, and if it does not say the owner will attend, the board may process it at a separate affidavit-only hearing (Tax Code 41.45(n)). | Texas appraisal review boards | "The board may consider an affidavit submitted under this section only if the property owner does not appear in person at the hearing." | [statute_or_rule](https://codes.findlaw.com/tx/tax-code/tax-sect-41-45/) | yes | Filing an affidavit does not waive the right to appear in person. |
| 9 | Travis County ARB (2025 procedures): a no-show protest is dismissed only if there is no evidence to consider, and a person appearing as agent must have a signed Appointment of Agent form on file. | Texas - Travis Appraisal Review Board | "The panel will dismiss the protest for failure to appear if it has no evidence to consider." | [statute_or_rule](https://traviscad.org/wp-content/uploads/2025-Travis-Appraisal-Review-Board-Formal-Hearing-Procedures_FINAL.pdf) | yes | Same document: "a person appearing as an agent for the property owner must have a signed Appointment of Agent form on file with TCAD" and the pamphlet warning "If you fail to appear, you may lose the right to be heard by the ARB on the protest and the right to appeal." 2025 edition; check the 2026 edition. |
| 10 | Harris County ARB (2026 rules): a protest may be dismissed for failure to appear, but if an affidavit is on file the hearing is held on the affidavit evidence instead. | Texas - Harris County Appraisal Review Board | "If an affidavit exists, a hearing shall be held on the basis of the evidence provided in the affidavit." | [statute_or_rule](https://hcad.org/assets/uploads/pdf/resources/2026/2026-Hearings-Rules-and-Procedures-Final.pdf) | yes | Section IV.2 also: "An account under protest may be dismissed for failure of the property owner or designated representative to appear". | 11 | Travis ARB's 2026 letter to agents: agent protests not completed by the Friday of the week after the scheduled date are dismissed for failure to appear, absent extenuating circumstances. | Texas - Travis Appraisal Review Board (agents, 2026) | "Protests not completed by Friday of the week following the scheduled date will be dismissed for failure to appear" | [statute_or_rule](https://traviscad.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-TARB-Agent-Letter.pdf) | yes | The letter puts tracking on the agent: "It is the agent's responsibility to track protests not completed".
