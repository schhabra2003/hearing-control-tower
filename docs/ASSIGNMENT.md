# The assignment

The take-home brief from FairAppeal. The firm, its people and every example message are fictional.

## Alice's Law Firm: hearing coordination

You are in charge of helping Alice scale her tech-enabled property-tax appeal firm through digital tools and processes. The firm operates in **10 counties across five states** and plans to expand across the country.

The government sets hearing dates. Sometimes the firm can plan dates with the local office. The firm arranges for part-time consultants to attend the hearings.

### How the firm works today

- Consultants run their own businesses. Some do not use digital calendars. Others may not share their calendars with the firm.
- The firm confirms who can attend each hearing or group of hearings.
- The firm tells the government who will attend. Each local office has its own process.
- The firm sends consultants the case files and hearing details.
- If someone cancels, the firm finds a replacement, sends the files and updates the government.
- Consultants may need to sign settlement or withdrawal agreements.

Only the government can approve a hearing date change. A change to a calendar does not change the hearing date.

The firm has basic workflows for case records, document collection and notifications. People still do much of the coordination.

### Your assignment

Help the firm handle more hearings with less human work and fewer errors.

- Choose the measures you would use to track success (KPIs).
- Set your priorities. Explain what you would do first, next and later, and why.
- Design the process for your first priority. Build a prototype of your solution and agents.
- Show where people are needed and how the firm knows the work is complete.

You can change the firm's existing workflows. Use Claude, Gemini, Codex or other tools. Assume the firm can provide the technical resources to build what you need. State your assumptions and the people, tools or budget you would need.

Use the access described in the examples. You can propose integrations. Simulate external messages and submissions.

### What to submit

- A **one-page write-up** with your measures, priorities and proposed process.
- A **prototype of your solution and agents**, with instructions so the firm can play around with it. Add a visual if it helps.

**The firm cares most about how you think about scaling the operation.** Show how your approach can handle more hearings and different local processes without adding the same amount of human work. Your prototype does not need to handle every situation.
